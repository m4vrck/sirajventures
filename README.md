# Siraj Ventures

> A Saudi market platform for business setup, residency, and growth. Founder-led. Built for the Kingdom's next decade.

## Overview
Siraj Ventures provides foreign founders, investors, and international enterprises with direct, on-the-ground intelligence and structured advisory to enter and scale in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.

## Features
- **Video Masterclasses**: Curated briefings from Fahad covering 100% foreign equity, MISA commercial licensing, capital requirements, and Saudi Premium Residency (SPPR).
- **Interactive Saudi Readiness Assessment**: 6-step diagnostic guiding founders to their optimal entry pathway.
- **Saudi Regulatory Ecosystem**: Interactive mapping of MISA, Ministry of Commerce, ZATCA, GOSI, Qiwa, Muqeem, and Balady.
- **Privacy-Enhanced Player**: Inline high-definition lightbox video player with zero third-party ads.
