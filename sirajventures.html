About Fahad + Siraj Ventures

Who am I trusting with my Saudi entry?

Fahad Siraj, the philosophy behind Siraj Ventures, and the standard we hold every engagement to — in one place, because separating the founder from the company would tell only half the story.

Fahad Siraj
Founder, Siraj Ventures

Fahad built his career on the ground in Saudi Arabia, navigating the historic transformation from legacy commercial systems to the modern Vision 2030 foreign investment era. Having guided international enterprises, manufacturers, and scale-ups through licensing, bank onboarding, and regulatory compliance, he founded Siraj Ventures to eliminate the bureaucratic friction and conflicting advice that slow founders down.

Most founders assume entering Saudi Arabia requires giving away half their business to a local sponsor or navigating bureaucratic dead-ends. The truth is that Saudi investment laws now empower 100% foreign ownership across most sectors. What determines success isn't connections — it is structural precision, speed of execution, and on-the-ground operational focus.

Why Saudi, Why Siraj Ventures

The case for the Kingdom — and for doing it with us.

Why Saudi Arabia

Under Vision 2030, the Kingdom is driving the largest economic diversification and infrastructure program on earth. Unprecedented public capital expenditure, regional headquarters incentives, and regulatory deregulation make early entry decisive for global and regional market leadership.

Why Siraj Ventures

Rather than managing fragmented law firms, government relations agents, and accounting vendors, Siraj Ventures acts as an accountable, founder-led execution partner from initial MISA licensing through to corporate banking, visas, and day-to-day commercial operations.

Siraj Ventures

Mission — To provide entrepreneurs and global enterprises with rapid, uncompromised, and fully compliant entry into the Saudi market with total ownership retention.

Vision — To be the premier founder-first gateway for international capital, industrial manufacturing, and tech innovation expanding into the Arabian Gulf.
